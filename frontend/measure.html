Open this page from the Core over HTTPS

Augmented reality only works over a secure connection (HTTPS). Open this page from your Core's https:// address (WAVR_MULTIDEVICE).

When the certificate warning appears, do not tap Continue blindly. Tap the warning, choose View certificate, and check that the SHA-256 fingerprint is the same one the Core's panel showed when it generated the pairing code. Only carry on if they match. If it is different, stop: somebody may be intercepting your network.

Pair this device first

To send a room, this device has to be paired at the Admin access level, not only for viewing.

  1. On the Core's panel, generate a pairing code at the Admin access level.
  2. Come back to the app on this phone and enter the code.
  3. Then open /measure.html again.

Wavr·Measure with your phone

Measure a room

Point the phone at the floor and mark each corner of the room. Wavr builds the polygon in metres and adds it to the house plan without erasing what you have already drawn.

●The camera is used by the browser alone, for AR tracking: no frame reaches Wavr and none leaves your network. The app keeps only the (x, y) coordinates in metres of the corners you mark.

It needs good light and a floor with some texture. Once you have saved, position the room in the Core's 2D editor.
