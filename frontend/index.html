    Runs an on-demand check of your gateway plus, if enabled on the hub, three public DNS resolvers (1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8, 9.9.9.9) to tell LAN problems from internet problems.

    This checks your gateway plus 3 public DNS servers outside your network (1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8, 9.9.9.9) — confirm?

    This check contacts your gateway and 3 public DNS servers outside your network — only when you click “Check now”, and only once per click.

    Deep network diagnosis

    diagnosing…

    Runs the full set of network/source/mDNS/inventory checks at once and reports what's healthy, what got auto-fixed (safe, local-only — never your router or other devices), and what still needs you. Also checks 3 public DNS resolvers, but only while egress is allowed on this hub — blocked egress means this stays a gateway/LAN-only diagnosis.

    Network sensing

    local sensing — nothing leaves your network

    These read standard network-discovery traffic already on your LAN (mDNS, UPnP, DHCP, etc.) to identify your devices better. Unlike the list above, these are not egress paths — they scan only your own network and nothing leaves it. Each collector is set from the hub's configuration — flipping one individually needs a hub restart, but the master switch above can pause all of them at once, right now.

    Routines

    when this happens, do that

    Privacy

    Privacy & security

    Wavr's contract with your Space

    Your data

    Exports, right here on this device, what this panel has already loaded: presence history, floor plan, and zones. Deleting data on the hub (“right to be forgotten”) needs a backend route — not in this version yet.

    Nothing leaves this Space by default. Four commitments hold for every Wavr installation:

    • Frames stay in memory only. Cameras process every frame in RAM and discard it — they never write to disk, never send video.
    • Vitals and positions exist live only. Breathing, heart rate, and movement paths exist in the live stream and are not retained beyond your hub.
    • All egress is opt-in. In the basic flow, only the narrator (History tab) sends data to the cloud — with confirmation, every time. Notifications (ntfy), MQTT, Home Assistant, MCP control, and a cloud Assistant engine are also optional egress paths from the hub: they ship off and only leave this Space if you turn them on.
    • Cameras start off. Every new camera (and every hub restart) starts off — turning it on is always your action.
